01 / Introduction

What is Montra Markets?

Montra Markets is a token-launch and decentralized trading ecosystem built around Elysium. The platform's launchpad model connects the creation of a token with the establishment of a market where the token can be traded.

For people searching for Montra Markets, Montramarkets, Montra Elysium or Montra Launchpad, this documentation explains the core concepts without assuming prior knowledge of liquidity pools and blockchain transactions.

Launching a token and having a functional market are not the same task. A smart contract can create a digital asset, but a market requires a trading mechanism, clear transaction rules and available liquidity. Understanding the full process is useful to creators, builders and traders alike.

03 / Architecture

How the pieces fit together

01

Blockchain environment

Elysium supplies an EVM-compatible execution environment. Its transactions use HYPE for network fees, and the chain settles to HyperEVM.

02

Token and market creation

A launch mechanism brings an ERC-20 token together with its initial onchain pool. Launch settings establish the terms of the market.

03

Liquidity and trading

Swaps interact with available pool liquidity. Execution price depends on pool state, trade size and applicable fees.

04

Discovery and verification

Before trading a newly launched asset, users should review its contract, token distribution, pool liquidity and onchain history.

04 / Essentials

Key terms to know

Liquidity pool

A smart-contract market that allows users to exchange assets against available liquidity.

ERC-20

A common fungible-token standard compatible with Ethereum-style smart contracts.

HYPE gas

The native asset used to pay for transactions on the Elysium network.

Uniswap v4

Pool infrastructure that supports configurable pool behavior through hooks.
